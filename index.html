Case study: CardioBot

A multi-model RAG assistant that answers clinical questions, reads EKG images, and handles dose calculations. It shows the full pattern: retrieval, multimodal input, and routing in one working app.

83%reduction in pre-arrival decision support time
895passages indexed from the 2023 ESC ACS Guidelines

The problem

Decision support needs to be fast and tied to published guidance. A single general-purpose model is neither reliably grounded nor equally good at text, images, and math.

The approach

Guideline passages live in ChromaDB for retrieval. Claude Sonnet handles questions, Gemini Vision reads EKG images, and GPT-4 handles calculations. LangGraph coordinates the workflow.
